Glaze layer

The primitive glaze-layer (card C-026): a red clay body under white slip, painted in cobalt and turquoise, with a ring cut through the slip to the clay, under a transparent glaze. The melted glaze levels itself, so it lies thick in hollows and thin over ridges, smooths over the body's fine texture, and carries the gloss; in the firing the pigment spreads into it, more where it lies thick. Set the levelling to 0 to see a glaze that only follows the body.
