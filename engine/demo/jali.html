Thick screen

The primitive thick-screen (card C-028): a jali cut through stone with depth. Seen at an angle, each opening shows part of its own inner wall. The sun behind it gets through only where its rays clear both faces, so the light on the wall inside is narrower than the openings, shifted by the sun's angle, and softer the further the wall is. Set the thickness to 0 to see a screen with no depth.
