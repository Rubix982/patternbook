Dye baths

The primitive dye-baths (card C-024): the cloth goes through its steps in order, and its colours come from that order. Blocks print a resist where dye must not go, and alum or iron where madder must take. Indigo dyes blue wherever no resist lies, darker with each dip. Madder dyes red over alum and black over iron, and only stains the rest, which washing removes. Move the indigo before the resist to see the red go dark; crack the mud resist to let indigo in as veins.
