Supplementary weft

The primitive supplementary-weft (card C-020): an extra, thicker weft laid over a woven ground along pattern lines, on the face only where the figure is, tied down by a binding warp end every few ends. Discontinuous (jamdani, Banarasi kadhua), continuous (floats on the back) or cutwork (the back's floats clipped); switch to the back to tell them apart.
