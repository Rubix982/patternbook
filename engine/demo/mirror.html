Mirror work

The primitive mirror-inset (card C-025): a disc of mirror held to the cloth by a square grid of threads and a ring of shisha stitch. Each stitch loops over the grid at the inner edge, runs out across the glass's rim, and goes into the cloth as a chain stitch. The ring covers the rim, so the glass shows through an opening smaller than itself that keeps something of the grid's square. Curved glass catches the lamp, each disc in its own place as it tilts.
