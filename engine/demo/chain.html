Chain stitch

The primitive chain-stitch (card C-022): a line of interlocking loops along a path, each link pinched where the thread goes into the cloth, open in its middle (the eye) and rounded at its front, where the next link comes up through it, as an aari hook makes it from above a stretched frame. Kashmiri zalakdozi fills a shape with concentric rings of it. The back shows straight stitches end to end; a running stitch on the same path shows no eyes.
