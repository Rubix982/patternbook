Kantha rows

The primitives contour-rows and quilt-ripple (card C-023): running stitch in rows that echo each motif's edge inward, rows of quilting across the ground around them, and the ripple the stitching pulls into layered cloth, lit from one side. Switch the rows to straight to see them ignore the contour; turn the ripple down to see the cloth lie flat.
