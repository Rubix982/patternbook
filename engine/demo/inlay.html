Stone inlay

The primitive inlay-cut (card C-027): pieces of stone cut to a floral design and set into recesses in marble, each fitted to the motif's own outline with a hair-thin seam. Each piece is cut from somewhere in its stone and turned so the stone's bands run along it. Turn the pieces at random to see the bands cross them, or cut the design as cells to see the outline lost.
