Counted darning

The primitive counted-darning (card C-021): floss stitches laid along the ground cloth's own picks or ends, over a number of its threads and under a few, row after row, staggered so the pick-ups make diagonals, in blocks of their own direction, as in phulkari and bagh. Untwisted floss shines along its filaments, so blocks darned across the light shine and blocks darned along it don't: move the light to see them swap. Turn "on the cloth's threads" off to see stitches on an even grid instead.
