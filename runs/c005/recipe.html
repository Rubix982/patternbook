Red Lentil Soup with Cumin & Lemon

Red lentils fall apart as they cook, so the soup thickens on its own with no blender needed. Toasted spices go in at the start, and a squeeze of lemon at the end brightens it.

Method

One pot, about 45 minutes start to finish.

  1. Soften the vegetables 8 min

    Warm the oil in a large pot over medium heat. Add the onion and carrot with a pinch of salt and cook, stirring now and then, until the onion is soft and translucent.

  2. Toast the aromatics 2 min

    Stir in the garlic, tomato paste, cumin, paprika and chili flakes. Cook until the paste darkens a shade and the spices smell toasted. Don't let the garlic brown.

  3. Simmer the lentils 20–25 min

    Add the rinsed lentils and stock. Bring to a boil, then lower to a gentle simmer, partly covered. Stir every few minutes; the lentils are done when they've collapsed and the soup looks creamy.

  4. Adjust the texture 2 min

    For a smoother soup, blend about a third of it and stir it back in. If it's too thick, add a splash of water. It will keep thickening as it sits.

  5. Finish and serve

    Take the pot off the heat and stir in the lemon juice. Taste for salt. Ladle into bowls and top with herbs and a drizzle of olive oil.

Storage

Keeps 4 days in the fridge and 3 months frozen. Thin with water when reheating.

Swaps

Yellow lentils work the same way. Brown or green lentils hold their shape and need about 10 more minutes.

Make it richer

Stir in a handful of spinach at the end, or swap a cup of the stock for coconut milk.

Serve with crusty bread or warm flatbread.
