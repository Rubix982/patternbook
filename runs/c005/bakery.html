Slow on purpose

How a loaf gets made

Every loaf on the shelf was started two days before you buy it. There's no shortcut in the schedule, and that's most of the flavour.

Day 1 · 16:00

Feed the levain

The starter, kept going since the day we opened, gets fresh flour and water.

Day 1 · 22:00

Mix & fold

Dough is mixed by hand and folded four times as it rises through the evening.

Overnight

Cold retard

Shaped loaves rest at 4 °C for fourteen hours, building sourness and blistered crust.

Day 2 · 05:00

Into the oven

Baked hot and dark in a wood-fired deck oven, then cooled before it reaches the counter.

Our story

A corner shop that never meant to grow

Maya started Crumb & Hearth in what used to be a shoe repair, with one second-hand oven and a starter she'd carried across two apartments. The plan was a few dozen loaves a day for the street.

Fifteen years on, the plan hasn't changed much. There are four of us now, the oven burns oak from a farm an hour north, and most of the people at the counter at 7:00 are the same ones who were there the first winter.

We still don't do wholesale. If you want our bread, you have to come by.

— Maya, Theo, Ines & June
