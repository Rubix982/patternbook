## Domains and families (from memory)

From memory, to be corrected as sources are read. Architecture goes down to elements; the other domains stop at
families for now, with an example or two.

### Architecture

- **Openings and screens:** jharokha; jali; carved doors and gateways (darwaza, pol); windows and shutters; wind
  catchers (the mangh of Hyderabad, Sindh).
- **Roofs and crowns:** domes (bulbous, double, ribbed); chhatri; the curved bangla roof (do-chala, char-chala,
  bangaldar); shikhara; vimana and gopuram; tiered timber roofs of the Himalaya and Nepal; finials (kalash).
- **Arches, columns and brackets:** pointed, four-centred, cusped, multifoil and ogee arches; trabeate openings;
  baluster columns; temple pillars; brackets (toda) and corbels; eaves (chhajja).
- **Spaces and plans:** courtyard (aangan, chowk); baradari; diwan-i-aam and diwan-i-khas; mandapa; hypostyle halls;
  charbagh; stepwells (baoli, vav); ghats; mosque courtyard (sahn).
- **Building types:** haveli; fort; palace; mosque; temple; gurdwara; tomb; stupa and vihara; dargah; caravanserai;
  bungalow; railway station; bazaar; village houses (the bhunga of Banni, the chaunra of Thar); houseboat.
- **Surfaces and ornament:** pietra dura (parchinkari); tile (kashi kari); fresco (naqqashi); stucco (ghalib kari);
  polished lime plaster (araish); mirror mosaic; stone relief; wood carving; painted facades; terracotta panels.
- **Materials as a look:** red sandstone; yellow sandstone; Makrana marble; small Lahori brick; terracotta; deodar;
  teak; mud and lime.
- **Lineages:** Indus; Buddhist (Mauryan, Gandhara); Hindu temple styles (Nagara, Dravida, Vesara, Kalinga,
  Maru-Gurjara, Hemadpanti); Jain; Sultanate (Delhi, Bengal, Gujarat, Deccan); Mughal; Rajput; Sikh; Kashmiri timber;
  Himalayan timber (kath-kuni); Newar; Sindhi (Makli, Shikarpur); Indo-Saracenic; colonial; Art Deco (Mumbai);
  modern (Chandigarh, Dhaka); vernacular.

### Other domains (families only, for now)

- **Textiles and dress:** printing and dyeing (block print, ajrak, dabu, bandhani, leheriya, kalamkari); weaving
  (brocade, jamdani, ikat, kani, khaddar, dari, carpets); embroidery (phulkari, chikankari, zardozi, mirror work,
  kantha, Kutch and Rabari, Balochi, sozni, aari, tilla, gota); garments and their drapes (shalwar kameez, kurta,
  angarkha, sherwani, sari, lehenga, gharara, dupatta, chador, turbans and caps); footwear (khussa, kolhapuri,
  Peshawari chappal).
- **Painting and depiction:** court painting schools; folk and ritual painting; scrolls and narrative painting;
  murals and frescoes; Company and academic painting; popular images (cinema posters, calendar art, truck and
  rickshaw painting).
- **Objects and crafts:** ceramics and tile; metalwork (bidri, brass, koftgari); woodwork (carving, inlay, lacquer);
  furniture (charpai, takht, jhoola); stone carving and inlay; paper (papier-mache, sanjhi, kites); lighting (diya,
  lanterns, camel-skin lamps); vessels (surahi, lota, matka); toys and puppets (kathputli, Channapatna).
- **Jewellery and adornment:** forms (jhumka, chandbali, tika, jhoomar, nath, haar, hansli, kada, bangles, payal);
  techniques (kundan, meenakari, polki, thewa, filigree, temple jewellery); materials (gold, silver, glass and lac
  bangles).
- **Body and floor:** mehndi; kolam, rangoli, alpana, mandana, aripan; tattoo (godna); the paranda.
- **Script and lettering:** scripts (Nastaliq, Naskh, Kufic, Shahmukhi, Sindhi, Devanagari, Gurmukhi, Bengali, Tamil,
  Sinhala); where they appear (inscriptions, manuscripts, signboards, truck lettering, wall writing).
- **Street, transport and signs:** trucks and buses; rickshaws; tongas; boats (shikara, river boats); hand-painted
  signs; cinema hoardings; matchbox and packaging labels; wedding lights and shamiana tents.
- **Gardens and water:** charbagh gardens; stepwells; tanks (kund); ghats; fountains and water channels.
- **Ritual and festival objects** (with care for what is sacred): toran door hangings; diyas and lanterns; kites;
  festival structures and processional objects.
