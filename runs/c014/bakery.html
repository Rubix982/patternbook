Wren Street, since 2009

Bread out of the oven at seven, and mostly gone by two.

Tuller’s is one deck oven, three bakers and a counter. Everything on the shelves was mixed the day before and baked in the room behind the till, starting at half past four in the morning.

What’s on today Order a whole cake

Open Tuesday to Sunday. Closed on Mondays, when the oven gets cleaned and the bakers sleep.

A day here

When things come out of the oven

Same order every day, give or take ten minutes. The two red times are the big bakes, when the bread on the shelf is still warm.

  1. 4:30

    Oven on. Yesterday’s dough comes out of the fridge.

  2. 7:00

    Doors open. Country loaves, baguettes and the first croissants.

  3. 9:00

    Cardamom knots and cinnamon buns.

  4. 10:30

    Second tray of croissants. The rye comes out.

  5. 11:00

    Cakes are cut and go on the counter.

  6. 14:00

    Whatever’s left is half price.

  7. 15:00

    Closed. Leftover bread goes to the Cole Lane community fridge.

Getting here

The 12 and 38 buses stop on Cole Lane, a minute’s walk north of the door. There’s a bike rack outside the window.

The entrance has no step and the door is wide enough for a double pram.
