Wi-Fi router troubleshooting

Start with the lights. They tell you which section to use.

Model WR-600   Guide 240117
1

Find the four lights

The lights are on the front edge, below the lid. Read them left to right.

  • 1Power
  • 2Internet — the link to your provider
  • 3Wi-Fi — the wireless network
  • 4Cable — something is plugged into a back port
2

Match the pattern

Find the row that looks like your router, then go to that letter.

A

No lights at all

The router is not getting power. Check both ends of the power cable.

1

Push the adapter all the way into the wall socket. Try another socket you know works.

2

Push the round plug firmly into the power port on the side of the router.

3

Wait one minute. Light 1 should turn green. If nothing lights, the adapter may be faulty.

B

No internet

Light 2 is amber or off. Most of the time a restart in the right order fixes this.

1

Follow the cable from your modem to the router's internet port (the one marked with a globe). Push it in until it clicks.

2

a Unplug the modem's power. b Then unplug the router's power.

3

Wait 30 seconds with both unplugged.

4

Plug the modem back in and wait 2 minutes, until its lights settle. Then plug in the router.

Still amber after 5 minutes? Your provider may have an outage. Check their status page on your phone's mobile data, or call them. Have the modem's serial number ready — it is on a label underneath.

C

Can't see the Wi-Fi network

Light 3 is off, or your network name is missing from the list on your phone.

1

Press the Wi-Fi button on the lid once. Don't hold it. Light 3 turns green within 10 seconds.

2

Turn the router over. The network name and password are printed on the label underneath.

3

Choose that name on your device and type the password exactly, including dashes. Capitals matter.

E

Slow, or keeps dropping

All lights are green, but some rooms get a weak signal. Where the router sits matters more than anything else.

Not in a cabinet, behind the TV, or on the floor. Wood, metal and water block the signal.

Out in the open, at chest height or higher, near the middle of your home. Antennas pointing straight up.

Keep it at least 1 m from a microwave, cordless phone base or baby monitor. They use the same radio band and cause drop-outs.

U

Light 1 blinking amber

The router is installing an update. This takes up to 10 minutes and the internet will be down meanwhile.

Do not unplug it. Cutting power during an update can leave the router unable to start. When light 1 turns steady green, it is done.

R

Factory reset — last resort

Only if nothing above helped, or you have lost the admin password.

1

With the router on, a push a straightened paperclip into the small reset hole on the side, b hold it for 10 seconds until all lights flash, then let go.

2

Wait 3 minutes for it to restart. Then join the network on the label (see C, step 3).

A reset erases your own network name, password and any settings. Every device in the house will need to reconnect.
