Restart vs. reset

These are different. A restart is safe and fixes most things. A reset erases your settings.

Restart (safe)

  1. Unplug the router's power cable. If you have a separate modem, unplug that too.
  2. Wait 30 seconds.
  3. Plug the modem in first and wait until its lights settle (about 2 minutes).
  4. Plug the router in. Wait about 3 minutes for the Internet light to turn solid green.

Your network name, password and settings are kept.

Factory reset (last resort)

  1. With the router on, press and hold the Reset hole with a straightened paperclip for 10 seconds, until all lights flash.
  2. Release and wait 3 minutes.
  3. Join the default network printed on the sticker under the router.
  4. Open http://192.168.1.1 and run setup again.
This erases everything: your Wi-Fi name and password, parental controls, port forwarding, and any login details your provider asked you to enter. Have those ready first.
