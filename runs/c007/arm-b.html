Halden AX3000 home router · Owner's guide Troubleshooting

Something's wrong with your Wi-Fi

Find what you can see: a light on the router, a message in the Halden app, or a problem on your phone or computer. Each entry lists the likely causes, quickest to check first. Work down the rows and stop when it's fixed.

This section only lists problems you can fix yourself. If you reach the last row of an entry and the problem is still there, contact Halden Support with the details that row asks for.

Before you start
  • Unplug the router's power adapter before moving it or changing cables at the back.
  • Don't open the router case. There are no parts inside you can repair.
  • Use only the power adapter that came in the box (12 V, 2 A).
Stop now

If any of these happen

  • You smell burning, or see smoke or sparks from the router or its adapter.
  • The case is too hot to keep your hand on.
  • Liquid has been spilled on the router, or it has been dropped and the case is cracked.
  • The power cable or adapter is damaged, swollen or melted.

Unplug the adapter from the wall socket. Don't plug it back in.

Don't try a restart or reset. Contact Halden Support and say which of the above happened. Your devices will need a different internet connection (for example a phone hotspot) until the router is replaced.

Problems you can see or notice

Tier tells you how soon to deal with it. Fix soon means some or all devices are offline. When convenient means it works, but badly. Not a fault means this is normal behaviour.

All the lights are off and nothing connectsFix soon

#CauseWhat to do
1The adapter is not pushed fully into the router or the wall socket.Push both ends in firmly. Wait 3 minutes.
2The wall socket has no power (switched off, or on a tripped circuit or power strip).Plug a lamp into the same socket to test it. Use a different socket if needed.
3The adapter isn't the one supplied with the router.Use the Halden 12 V, 2 A adapter from the box.
Still no lightsContact Halden Support. Have the serial number from the label underneath ready.

Devices connect to Wi-Fi, but nothing loads on any of themFix soon

#CauseWhat to do
1Your internet provider has an outage in your area.Check your provider's outage page or app using mobile data. If there's an outage, wait.
2The modem and router lost sync after a power cut or line drop.Restart both, modem first. See Restarting the modem and router.
3The cable between modem and router is in the wrong port.On the router, the cable from the modem must be in the port marked with the globe (), not ports 1–3.
4Your provider needs a username and password (PPPoE), and it isn't set or has changed.See message PPPoE sign-in failed.
Still no internetConnect a computer directly to the modem with a cable. If it still has no internet, contact your internet provider. If it does, contact Halden Support.

One device can't connect, but the others canFix soon

#CauseWhat to do
1The device's Wi-Fi or airplane mode is set wrong.Turn the device's Wi-Fi off and on again. Check airplane mode is off.
2The device saved an old password for your network.On the device, choose "Forget this network", then join again with the current password.
3The device is blocked or paused in the Halden app (parental controls).In the app, open Devices, find it, and choose Unpause or Unblock.
4The device is older and doesn't support WPA3 security.In the app, set Wi-Fi › Security to WPA2/WPA3 mixed.
Still can't connectRestart the device. If it still fails, contact the device's maker; the router is working for your other devices.

My Wi-Fi name doesn't appear in the list of networksFix soon

#CauseWhat to do
1The router is still starting (Power light pulsing).Wait until the Power light is solid white.
2A Wi-Fi schedule has turned Wi-Fi off for the night.In the app, open Wi-Fi › Schedule and turn it off or change the times.
3The network name is set to hidden.In the app, open Wi-Fi › Network name and turn on Show network name.
4The device only supports 2.4 GHz and the 2.4 GHz band is switched off.In the app, open Wi-Fi › Bands and turn on 2.4 GHz.
Still not listedRestart the router (see Restarting the router). If the Wi-Fi light stays off, contact Halden Support.

The internet is slowWhen convenient

#CauseWhat to do
1Another device is downloading or uploading something large (backups, game updates).In the app, open Devices and sort by Usage. Pause the busy device or wait.
2You're far from the router or there are thick walls between you.Test next to the router. If it's fast there, the problem is range. See Choosing where the router sits.
3You're on the 2.4 GHz band, which is slower.In the app, check Devices › [your device] › Band. Move closer so it can switch to 5 GHz.
4Neighbouring networks are using the same channel.In the app, open Wi-Fi › Channel and choose Auto, then Rescan now.
5Your plan's speed is lower than you expect.Run a speed test with a computer plugged into the router by cable. Compare it with the speed on your bill.
Still slow next to the router, by cableContact your internet provider with the cable speed-test result. If cable speed is fine but Wi-Fi next to the router is under half of it, contact Halden Support.

Wi-Fi keeps dropping out every so oftenWhen convenient

#CauseWhat to do
1The router is in a cupboard, behind a TV, or next to a microwave or cordless phone base.Move it into the open, away from those. See Choosing where the router sits.
2The router is overheating because its vents are covered.Leave 10 cm clear on all sides. Don't stack anything on top.
3The router's software is out of date.Install the latest update. See Updating the router's software.
4The internet itself is dropping (Internet light turns amber when it happens).Treat it as an internet problem: see No internet on any device.
Still droppingIn the app, open Help › Send diagnostics just after a drop, then contact Halden Support with the reference number it shows.

I don't know the Wi-Fi passwordWhen convenient

#CauseWhat to do
1It was never changed from the one the router came with.Read the password on the label underneath the router, next to Wi-Fi key.
2It was changed, and you have the Halden app signed in on a phone.In the app, open Wi-Fi › Share. The password is shown and can be shared as a QR code.
Neither worksReset the router to factory settings. This erases all your settings. See Resetting to factory settings.

The Power light has been pulsing white for more than 10 minutesFix soon

#CauseWhat to do
1A large software update is still installing.Wait 10 more minutes. Don't unplug it during this time.
2Start-up stalled.Restart the router once. See Restarting the router.
Still pulsing after a restartContact Halden Support. Say how long it has been pulsing and whether an update was running.

The Power light is solid redFix soon

#CauseWhat to do
1A one-off start-up error.Restart the router once. See Restarting the router.
2The router is too hot (it shows red to protect itself).Unplug it, let it cool for 30 minutes in an open spot, then plug it in again. If the case was too hot to touch, follow Stop now instead.
Still redContact Halden Support. The router needs to be replaced; resetting won't help.

The top of the router feels warmNot a fault

Normal
The router uses the top of its case to get rid of heat, so warm to the touch is expected, especially when it's busy. If it's too hot to keep your hand on, follow Stop now.

The router seems to restart by itself during the nightNot a fault

Normal
Automatic updates install between 2 and 4 a.m. and restart the router, which takes about 5 minutes. To change the time, open Settings › Updates in the app.

My network appears twice, or with "-5G" on the endNot a fault

Normal
The router broadcasts on two bands. You can join either. To show one name that switches band automatically, turn on Wi-Fi › Bands › Combine bands in the app.

Wi-Fi speed is a bit lower than my plan, even next to the routerNot a fault

Normal, within limits
Wi-Fi usually gives 70–90% of the speed you get by cable. Below half by cable speed is a fault: see The internet is slow.

The lights are off, but the Wi-Fi worksNot a fault

Normal
Settings › Lights is set to Off or Night only. Press the Power light once to show the lights for 30 seconds.

Messages in the Halden app

Messages as they appear in the app, in alphabetical order. Match the text exactly; the code in brackets helps Support.

Another device is using this IP address (E-21)When convenient

#CauseWhat to do
1A device was given a fixed address by hand that the router also gives out.In the app, open Devices, tap the device named in the message, and choose Use automatic address.
2There are two routers on the same network (for example, your provider's modem also has routing on).Ask your provider to set their modem to bridge mode.
Message comes backContact Halden Support with the two device names shown in the message.

Couldn't install update (E-12)When convenient

#CauseWhat to do
1The internet dropped during the download.Tap Try again once the Internet light is solid white.
2The router was unplugged or restarted during the update.Leave it plugged in and tap Try again. Your settings are kept.
Fails twiceContact Halden Support. The router keeps running on its current software in the meantime.

Internet cable unplugged (E-01)Fix soon

#CauseWhat to do
1The cable from the modem is loose or out.Push it into the globe port until it clicks. Do the same at the modem end.
2The modem is off.Check the modem's lights. Turn it on and wait 3 minutes.
3The cable is damaged (bent pins, broken clip).Try a different network cable.
Still shows the messageContact Halden Support. Say whether the small light next to the globe port is on.

Incorrect password (E-31)When convenient

#CauseWhat to do
1A letter is in the wrong case, or 0 and O, l and 1 were mixed up.Type it again, checking each character. Passwords are case-sensitive.
2The password was changed and the device remembers the old one.Forget the network on the device and join again.
Don't know the current passwordSee I don't know the Wi-Fi password.

No response from router (E-40)When convenient

#CauseWhat to do
1Your phone is on mobile data or a different Wi-Fi network.Join your home Wi-Fi, then reopen the app.
2The router is restarting.Wait until the Power light is solid white, then pull down to refresh.
Still no responseRestart the router (see Restarting the router). If the app still can't reach it, contact Halden Support.

PPPoE sign-in failed (E-05)Fix soon

#CauseWhat to do
1The username or password from your provider was typed wrong.In the app, open Internet › Connection type and re-enter them from your provider's letter or account page.
2Your provider doesn't use PPPoE.Set Connection type to Automatic (DHCP).
Still failsContact your internet provider and ask them to confirm your PPPoE username and password.

Procedures

The longer fixes that the tables above point to.

Restarting the router

  1. Unplug the power adapter from the back of the router.
  2. Wait 30 seconds.
  3. Plug it back in. Wait until the Power light is solid white (up to 3 minutes).

Your settings are kept. Don't use the Reset hole for a restart; that erases settings.

Restarting the modem and router

  1. Unplug the router, then unplug the modem.
  2. Wait 1 minute.
  3. Plug in the modem only. Wait until its internet or online light is steady (up to 5 minutes).
  4. Plug in the router. Wait until its Internet light is solid white.

Choosing where the router sits

  1. Put it near the middle of your home, at shelf height or higher, in the open.
  2. Keep it at least 1 m from microwaves, cordless phone bases, baby monitors, fish tanks and large mirrors.
  3. Stand it upright with 10 cm clear on all sides.
  4. After moving it, open Wi-Fi › Coverage check in the app and walk to the rooms you use most.

Updating the router's software

  1. In the app, open Settings › Updates and tap Check now.
  2. If an update is listed, tap Install. The Power light pulses while it installs.
  3. Don't unplug the router until the Power light is solid white again (up to 8 minutes).

Resetting to factory settings

This erases your network name, password and all settings. Every device will need to join again.

  1. With the router on, find the small hole marked Reset on the back.
  2. Press and hold inside it with a straightened paper clip for 10 seconds, until the Power light flashes red.
  3. Let go. Wait until the Power light is solid white (up to 5 minutes).
  4. Join the network printed on the label underneath, and set it up again in the Halden app.
