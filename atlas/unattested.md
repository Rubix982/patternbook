# Items from the first pass that no source attested

_C-015, 2026-11-22. Kept out of the atlas until a source names them; not deleted. "Not found" is not "doesn't exist": several were never queried for lack of budget._

## Architecture; Gardens and water

Twenty-two sketch items found no source in this pass, plus one found only in part (the mangh's region). "Not queried" means the 80-fetch budget ran out before a direct query; those items are unconfirmed, not disproved.

- **pol (as a gate)**: title "Pol (gate)" is missing. "Pol (housing)" exists but means a Gujarat housing cluster (listed above as its own element).
- **carved doors** (as distinct from gateways): no element-level source among the categories, link lists or extracts fetched.
- **windows and shutters** (generic): nothing subcontinent-specific beyond the Newar window in the categories and link lists.
- **mangh, region only (partial)**: "Mangh" redirects to Windcatcher, so the term is attested. The search "mangh Hyderabad Sindh windcatcher" returned 0 hits, so Hyderabad (Sindh) is not confirmed.
- **double dome; ribbed dome**: not queried by title. The South Asian domes intro and the link lists show only onion/bulbous dome, semi-dome and Persian domes.
- **ogee arch** (as an arch type): appears only inside the gavaksha intro ("an ogee, circular or horseshoe arch" motif).
- **trabeate opening**: no subcontinental source. Corbel arch is listed instead.
- **baluster column**: the link lists show only "Balustrade" (Pakistani architecture).
- **toda (bracket)**: title "Toda (architecture)" is missing. "Toda people", linked from Indian vernacular architecture, is an ethnic group. Corbel is linked from Mughal architecture, and pekada covers Sri Lankan bracket capitals.
- **aangan, chowk** (as courtyard names): both titles are disambiguation pages with no architectural sense.
- **railway station** (as a building type): not queried. Only "Category:Railway stations in Bangladesh opened in the 21st century" appeared in a listing.
- **chaunra of Thar**: title "Chaunra" is missing. The search "chaunra OR chaunro Thar" gave no relevant hits.
- **houseboat**: title "Kashmiri houseboat" is missing. The "Houseboat" intro is generic and does not mention the subcontinent.
- **naqqashi** (the name): title "Naqqashi" is missing. "Nagash painting", linked from Mughal architecture, redirects to Al-Qatt Al-Asiri, an Arabian wall-painting style. Fresco itself is attested.
- **mirror mosaic**: title "Aina-kari" is missing. "Ayeneh-kari" is Iranian, "Sheesh Mahal" is a disambiguation page of building names and "Thikri" is a town. Mud mirror art (Kutch, Sindh) is a different technique and is listed separately.
- **stone relief; wood carving; painted facades**: not queried by title. They appear only as label-only Wikidata items ("Indian Wood Carving architecture style", "Shekhawati architecture style") that have no article.
- **yellow sandstone**: not found. "Red sandstone" redirects to Sandstone.
- **deodar** (as building timber): the "Deodar" article exists, but no fetched text ties it to building.
- **teak**: not queried.
- **Shikarpur** (as a Sindhi lineage): "Shikarpur" is a place article. Sindh is covered only through the Samma (Makli) category and the Chaukhandi tombs.
- Also not found: the brief's example branch "timber jharokhas of Sindh". The Jharokha intro names Rajput architecture only.

## Textiles and dress; Jewellery and adornment; Body and floor

- **Swat embroidery.** Title check for "Swat embroidery" returned missing. A Wikipedia search for "Swat embroidery" returned the Swat princely state, Swat Museum, Chitral Museum and unrelated pages, with no embroidery article. Category:Pakistani embroidery (5 members) and the Khyber Pakhtunkhwa clothing article have no Swat entry.
- **Polki.** The title "Polki" redirects to "Diamonds as an investment" (not read). Category:Jewellery of India, Kundan, Bangle, Khara dupatta and the jewellery section of Crafts of Sindh do not name it.
- **Tilla work on khussa** (as a combination). Tilla is attested on its own (Kashmiri "tille" in Kashmiri handicrafts; Punjabi "tila-kar" in Embroidery of India) and so is the khussa, but the Khussa article lists embroidery, brass nails, cowries, pom-poms, mirrors, bells and beads, not tilla. Titles "Tilla embroidery" and "Tilla work" are missing.
- Attested only in part: **Kanchipuram borders.** Kanchipuram silk saris are attested (GI register, Category:Silk in India); the border as a named feature was not checked because the Kanchipuram silk sari article was not read.
- Attested as materials, not as elements: **gold, silver.** Crafts of Sindh names gold, silver, bronze, copper, brass, ivory, shell and glass as jewellery materials. No source treats them as a named kind of made thing, so they have no row; Hupari Silver Craft and Kolkatti Jewellery (GI) are the nearest material-named items.
- Weakly attested: **chandbali.** The only source naming it is an image caption in the Earring article ("Chandbali earrings, Asansol, West Bengal, India"). The title "Chandbali" is a town in Odisha.
- **Chador** is attested as "chadar" (Balochistan in Pakistani clothing; an Assamese drape in the Sari article). The article titled "Chador" is the Iranian garment, listed under Boundary.
