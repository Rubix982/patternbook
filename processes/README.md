# processes

The physical processes behind every look in patternbook: how something was made, what that process can't do, what
it always gets slightly wrong, and the maths that models it (METHOD step 2c). `docs/architecture.md` section 7b
explains why they live here, at the top level.

A process belongs to no single tradition and to neither shelf. Resist dyeing makes Java's batik, Sindh's ajrak and
Rajasthan's dabu. A press's ink spread is the same physics on a 1950s field magazine and on a block-printed cotton.
So the pages shelf's print treatments, the atlas's crafts, and the treatments and generators all point here.

## What is here

All of patternbook's knowledge of how looks are made sits in this folder, and nowhere else:

- `<process>.md`: one page per process. Sections 1-7 (the process, its constraints, its imperfections, the maths, the
  dials, its variants, what it makes) are written by hand from sources; section 8 carries every model of the process in
  full (its account, its steps, its equations with the primitives they apply, its dials, its history, its credit),
  generated by `tools/process_models.mjs` between markers; patternbook's own treatments' theory sits on the page of the
  process it models (`letterpress.md`: `press`; `risograph-printing.md`: `riso`; `halftone-screening.md`: `halftone`;
  `pen-and-ink-drawing.md`: `hatch`).
- `primitives.md`: the operations that recur across processes, each written once with its maths, and which models and
  processes apply it; patternbook's own primitives (`weave-draft`, `supplementary-weft`, `counted-darning`,
  `chain-stitch`, `contour-rows`, `quilt-ripple`, and the cards that follow) with their checks.
- `foundations.md`: the shared tools of patternbook's own treatments.
- `index.md`: generated. Every process with its models, and the entries that model no physical process.
- `models/`: the same models in machine-readable form (`lumenary/<id>.json`), and the classification and tags they are
  built from (`lumenary-map.tsv`, `lumenary-equation-tags.tsv`).
- `sources/lumenary/`: Lumenary as received, unchanged and credited: his theory data (`data/`) and his theory pages as
  captured from his site (`theory-pages/`). The upstream archive; read the process pages instead.

The code that runs these lives elsewhere: the engine in `engine/` (its base is Lumenary's engine; patternbook's process models are in `engine/core/`),
the treatments that apply looks to pages in `treatments/`.

## Where models come from

Any implementation of a process is a model of it, credited to whoever made it:

- **patternbook's own treatments and primitives** (`treatments/`, `engine/`). Their theory is on the process pages and
  in `primitives.md`; `treatments/theory/` keeps one-line pointers to where each note went.
- **Lumenary** by Mursal Furqan Kumbhar (https://github.com/mursalfk/lumenary). His engine is the base of `engine/`
  (see its `NOTICE.md`); his theory data and theory pages are in `sources/lumenary/`, as received. Each process page
  carries his models in full, credited; improvements are offered back to him.
- **Papers and books**, cited through `references/references.md`.

## Rules

- A process page is written from sources and from the process's own physics, not from how a look appears.
- Every equation says where it came from: a model's code, a paper, or derived here.
- The variants section is where traditions differ: the same process, different rules (wax, mud, or a paste with
  mordants as the resist).
- Atlas elements link here through their `processes:` field. A process page never claims an element that the atlas
  doesn't list.
