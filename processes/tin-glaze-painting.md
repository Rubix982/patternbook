## Notes

- Hala and Multan blue pottery (`lumenary:hala-blue-pottery`) is filed here because Lumenary's model paints on a white
  glaze, and because the brief for C-017 named it as a variant of tin-glaze painting. That example was wrong as stated:
  this ware is usually described as painted on a white slip under a clear alkaline glaze, not on tin glaze. Check the
  sources when this page is written; it may belong under its own process (underglaze painting on slip).
